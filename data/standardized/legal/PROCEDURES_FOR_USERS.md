# PROCEDURES FOR USERS

## Part B – Supplementary provisions

### Appendix 1: PROCEDURES FOR USERS

*(This Appendix is attached hereto and an integral part of the Terms of Use)*

*Step 1: Download and install the Application to your phone*

*Step 2: Create an account*

Users follow the instructions, providing information about their full name and phone number; An activation code will be automatically sent by the system to the registered phone number; Enter the activation code into the Application and start using the service. 

*Step 3:*

*For Green SM Bike: Enter trip itinerary information on the Applcation*

*For Green SM Express: Enter order and itinerary information on the Application*

The user enters all of the following information into the corresponding fields: Place of pick-up, place of receipt; Shipper information, consignee information; type of service; mass. 

*For Green SM Car: Enter trip itinerary information on the Applcation.*

*For Green SM Food: Select the Store and enter the Order Information on the Application.*

*For Green SM Delivery: Enter the Order details and route in the Application.*

The User must provide the following information in the corresponding fields: Pickup point, delivery point; sender’s information, recipient’s information; type of goods; weight. 

*Step 4:*  

Other Information: The User shall select the method of payment (cash, card, or any other payment method available on the Application), promotion code (if any), and other notes in order to obtain the determined price.. 
 
*For Green SM Bike*: The User shall confirm the Product Order. 

Product Order shall be transmitted to the nearest Partner, and the Partner shall move to the Pick-up Point to pick up the User. 

Within thirty (30) seconds after the Partner confirms to perform the User’s Product Order and the User/Partner does not cancel the Order, the User agrees that a legal agreement (the Contract) in electronic form between the User and the Partner is concluded.  

*For Green SM Express*: The User shall confirm the Product Order. 

Product Order shall be transmitted to the nearest Partner, and the Partner shall move to the Pick-up Point to receive the Goods and deliver to the Recipient.

Within thirty (30) seconds after the Partner confirms to perform the User’s Product Order and the User/Partner does not cancel the service Order, the User agrees that a legal agreement (the Contract) in electronic form between the User and the Partner is concluded.

*For Green SM Car*: The User shall confirm the Product Order. 

Product Order shall be transmitted to the Partner, and the Partner shall move to the Pick-up Point to pick up the User. 

Within thirty (30) seconds after the Partner confirms to perform the User’s Product Order and the User/Partner does not cancel the Order, the User agrees that the legal agreement (the Contract and Transportation contract) in electronic form between the User and the Partner is concluded. 

*For Green SM Food*: The User shall confirm the Product Order. 

Product Order shall be transmitted to the Partner, and the Partner shall move to the Point of Sale to collect the Goods and deliver them to the Delivery Point designated by the User. 

By confirming the Product Order, the User agrees that the legal agreement (the Contract and Transportation contract) in electronic form between the User and the Partner is concluded. 

*For Green SM Delivery: The User confirms the order for the Product:*

Product Order will be forwarded to the nearest Partner, and the Partner will proceed to the Pickup Point to receive the Goods and deliver to the Recipient.

Within 30 seconds after the Partner confirms execution of the User’s Product order and neither the User nor the Partner cancels the service order, the User agrees that legally binding agreement (the Contract and Transportation Document) in electronic form between the User and the Partner is concluded. 

*Step 5: Payment* 

The User shall make payment in accordance with the method displayed on the Application as selected (including cash, Card, or any other payment method available on the Application) after using the Services and Products. 

*Step 6: Evaluation*

The User evaluate the Service and send questions, complaints/reflections on Service quality (if any).
