1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1. “Fee Summary Table” means the summary of Service Fees and other costs incurred for successfully completed Trips within each Settlement Period. 

1.2. “Trip” means a transportation journey defined by a pick-up point and one or more drop-off points, or a point of receiving and delivering goods, as established between the Driver and the Customer or User via the APP.

1.3. “Service” means the transportation service connected via the APP, including but not limited to (a) passenger transport by electric car, (b) passenger transport by electric motorcycle, (c) cargo transport by electric motorcycle. GSM may adjust the Services and publish updates on the APP and/or GSM Website.

1.4. “Settlement Period” means the period for aggregating and reconciling Service Fees, calculated from 00:00 on the 23rd of the previous month to 22:59 on the 22nd of the settlement month.

1.5. “User” means a person authorized by the Customer to directly use the Service under the Contract between GSM and the Customer.

1.6. “Vehicle” means an electric car with fewer than 9 (nine) seats and/or an electric motorcycle owned and/or commercially operated by GSM or GSM’s cooperating partners qualified to operate transportation services under applicable law, which is used or approved by GSM to provide the Service.

1.7. “Management Account” means the account identifying the Customer provided by GSM for the Customer to manage and monitor Application Accounts and the Green SM Cards of Users.

1.8. “Application Account” means the account identifying a User, established and managed under the Application Account. For clarity, the User will download and install the APP, register, and activate the account following instructions on the APP.

1.9. “Driver” means the person operating the Vehicle to perform a Trip, having valid legal qualifications to drive, registered, and approved by GSM to provide the Service.

3. SERVICE FEES, RECONCILIATION, AND PAYMENT

3.1. Service Fees

3.1.1. The Service Fee for each Trip shall be determined according to GSM’s general policy effective at the time for each type of Service and is displayed on the APP when the User submits a service request. For clarity, when the User agrees to start the Trip, it is understood that the Customer/User accepts the Service Fee calculated by the APP, has read, understood, and agrees to TOU.

3.1.2. Service Fees:
- Include VAT as prescribed by law and platform fees (if any).
- Exclude tolls, ferry fees, and parking fees.

3.2. Reconciliation

3.2.1. Within 04 working days after the end of a Reconciliation Period, GSM shall prepare and send by email to the Customer’s point of contact a Fee Summary of the immediately preceding Reconciliation Period. The Fee Summary is determined based on completed Trips, Service Fees, and the fees specified in Section 3.1.2 of this GT, as recorded in GSM’s system. 

3.2.2. The Customer has 02 days from receipt of the Fee Summary to review and respond by email to GSM. The Customer has the right to access the list of Trips and corresponding fees and costs via the Management Account. If GSM does not receive a response from the Customer within this timeframe, it shall be considered that the Customer agrees with the Fee Summary, and GSM will issue a VAT invoice to the Customer. 

3.2.3. The Customer’s confirmation (or failure to respond within the specified timeframe) of the Fee Summary as set forth in Section 3.2.2 shall be deemed as the Parties having accepted the Service for the corresponding period. 

3.2.4. If the Parties cannot agree on all figures in the Fee Summary within the timeframe specified in Section 3.2.2, GSM will issue an invoice based on the figures in the Fee Summary sent by GSM. The Parties shall continue to discuss any discrepancies in the next Reconciliation Period, and any differences (if any) shall be offset in the next Reconciliation Period and the VAT invoice adjusted accordingly.

3.3. Payment
The User chooses to use the Service under the “Corporate” profile on the APP and, on behalf of the Customer, shall pay the Service Fees and any other arising costs immediately after the completion of the Trip.
