Accordingly, Partners and/or Green SM have the right to inspect the Goods if such suspicions arise.4. Compensation Liability4.1. Value of Goods4.1.1. The value of the Goods determined under this Section serves as the basis for calculating the compensation liability of Green SM/Partners under Section 4.3 below. The value of the Goods shall be the amount stated on a valid invoice issued by the seller to the User at the time of purchase of the Goods (“Invoice”), provided that the description of the Goods on the Invoice corresponds with the description that the User entered in the Order, and the Invoice/document was issued prior to the time the User submitted the Order.

4.1.2. A valid invoice means: 

- A value-added tax (“VAT”) invoice that meets the legally required conditions and is valid under applicable law, if the seller is a business that declares VAT under the credit method; 
- A sales invoice if the seller is a business that declares VAT under the direct method; or a comparative value statement; 
- Customs declaration documents if the Goods are imported from abroad into Vietnam.4.2. User’s Compensation Liability:Provided that Green SM is able to establish the User's fault and the resulting damage. The User shall be liable to compensate Green SM, the Partners, and any related Parties (if applicable) for all damages arising in the following cases:

- Damages occurring directly or indirectly due to the fault of the User; 
- Goods that fall under the list of prohibited or restricted goods for business/transportation under applicable law; 
- Goods that are improperly packaged, resulting in breakage and affecting the Goods of other customers.4.3. Compensation Liability of Green SM/Partner:4.3.1. Compensation Principles 

Green SM/Partner shall compensate according to the following principles:

(i) Compensate for direct damages based on agreements related to the provision and use of Products between Green SM/Partner and the User. No compensation shall be made for indirect damages, non-contractual losses, or lost profits arising from the non-performance of Products.
(ii) Goods that are completely lost, damaged, or swapped shall be compensated according to the regulations applicable to each type. 
(iii) The Customer/User must provide evidence demonstrating the fault and the damages, such as video recordings or images of the continuous packing process showing the type of Goods, quantity, weight, and other relevant details of the Goods that the Customer/User handed over to the Partner.
(iv) No compensation shall be made in the following cases:

- Absence of documents confirming the acceptance of the Goods;
- Absence of documents proving the use of the Products;
- Damages occurring entirely due to the User’s breach of the v or due to the natural characteristics or inherent defects of the Goods;
- The User fails to prove the sending, mishandling, or damage of the Goods;
- Goods have been delivered and the recipient did not raise any objection upon receipt;
- Goods are confiscated or destroyed in accordance with the law;
- The User fails to comply with the complaint procedures as stipulated by Green SM/Partner and the law.
