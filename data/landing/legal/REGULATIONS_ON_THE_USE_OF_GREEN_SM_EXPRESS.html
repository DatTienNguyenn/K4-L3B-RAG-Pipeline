APPENDIX 4 – REGULATIONS ON THE USE OF GREEN SM EXPRESS (This Appendix is attached hereto and an integral part of the Terms of Use) 1. For Users/Customers 3.1.1. Must comply with and fully adhere to the Green SM Terms and Policies when using the Services and Products; 3.1.2. Must not send any parcel containing the following items: (a) Narcotics, psychoactive substances; (b) Precious metals (gold, silver, etc., in the form of bars, ingots, coins, etc.); (c) Hazardous goods; (d) Firearms, weapons, ammunition, military technical equipment; (e) Tobacco products; (f) Perishable goods (items whose original condition or properties may be damaged due to excessive changes in temperature, humidity, or time), except flowers and certain food products; (g) Obscene and reactionary publications, printed materials or documents against public security; (h) Flammable, explosive, unhygienic, or environmentally polluting items or substances; (i) Items or goods prohibited from circulation or trade under applicable laws; (j) Live animals; (k) Animal furs; (l) Items, publications, or goods prohibited from import into Vietnam; (m) Items, publications, or goods prohibited from import into Vietnam; (n) Antiques (fragile); (o) Addictive substances; (p) Other goods as prescribed by law or as updated by Green SM. 3.1.3. Represent and warrant that You are the owner of the Goods or an authorized representative of the owner and that You accept the Terms or are authorized to accept the Terms on behalf of the owner. 3.1.4. Be responsible for the accuracy and completeness of the Order and Goods information entered by You on the Application. Green SM and the Partner shall not be liable for any delayed delivery or failure to deliver caused by incorrect Order information provided by You. 3.1.5. Represent and warrant that You are duly authorized by the recipient to provide the recipient’s information to Green SM and the Partner via the Application or other means. 3.1.6. Prior to the commencement of delivery, You must inform the Partner of any specific precautions necessary to handle the Goods in accordance with their nature. 3.1.7. Represent and warrant that You have packaged the Goods properly and in accordance with required standards, including neat arrangement, labeling, and marking. 3.1.8. Provide full and feasible instructions to the Partner; otherwise, the Partner may refuse to deliver the Goods. 3.1.9. Be responsible for any delays in receipt, or any loss, damage, contamination, pollution, confiscation, or any impact, direct or indirect, on any individual or organization caused by You. 3.1.10. A shipment shall be accepted only if all of the following conditions are met: - It does not contain any items or Goods specified in clause 3.1.2; - Complete sender and recipient information is provided on the shipment, along with any other information required by the Application; - The Partner has confirmed acceptance of the Order from the Application; - It has been packaged and wrapped as required (if applicable). 3.2. For Partners and Green SM 3.2.1. Partners and related parties must comply with and fully implement the Terms and Policies of Green SM as published. 3.2.2. Partners, when performing delivery requests for Goods, must act conscientiously, strictly comply with traffic regulations, and ensure safety for themselves and others. 3.2.3. Partners, as the operators of the vehicles, shall be legally responsible for ensuring the safety of the vehicles. 3.2.4. Partners and Green SM have the right to refuse to provide the Product if the Goods are oversized and the Partner assesses that they may violate traffic laws applicable to vehicles on the road and/or do not meet safety standards; or if there is suspicion that the Goods belong to the category of prohibited items or items not permitted for transport under applicable law. Accordingly, Partners and/or Green SM have the right to inspect the Goods if such suspicions arise. 4. Compensation Liability 4.1. Value of Goods 4.1.1. The value of the Goods determined under this Section serves as the basis for calculating the compensation liability of Green SM/Partners under Section 4.3 below. The value of the Goods shall be the amount stated on a valid invoice issued by the seller to the User at the time of purchase of the Goods (“Invoice”), provided that the description of the Goods on the Invoice corresponds with the description that the User entered in the Order, and the Invoice/document was issued prior to the time the User submitted the Order. 4.1.2. A valid invoice means: - A value-added tax (“VAT”) invoice that meets the legally required conditions and is valid under applicable law, if the seller is a business that declares VAT under the credit method; - A sales invoice if the seller is a business that declares VAT under the direct method; or a comparative value statement; - Customs declaration documents if the Goods are imported from abroad into Vietnam. 4.2. User’s Compensation Liability: Provided that Green SM is able to establish the User's fault and the resulting damage. The User shall be liable to compensate Green SM, the Partners, and any related Parties (if applicable) for all damages arising in the following cases: - Damages occurring directly or indirectly due to the fault of the User; - Goods that fall under the list of prohibited or restricted goods for business/transportation under applicable law; - Goods that are improperly packaged, resulting in breakage and affecting the Goods of other customers. 4.3. Compensation Liability of Green SM/Partner: 4.3.1. Compensation Principles Green SM/Partner shall compensate according to the following principles: (i) Compensate for direct damages based on agreements related to the provision and use of Products between Green SM/Partner and the User. No compensation shall be made for indirect damages, non-contractual losses, or lost profits arising from the non-performance of Products. (ii) Goods that are completely lost, damaged, or swapped shall be compensated according to the regulations applicable to each type. (iii) The Customer/User must provide evidence demonstrating the fault and the damages, such as video recordings or images of the continuous packing process showing the type of Goods, quantity, weight, and other relevant details of the Goods that the Customer/User handed over to the Partner. (iv) No compensation shall be made in the following cases: - Absence of documents confirming the acceptance of the Goods; - Absence of documents proving the use of the Products; - Damages occurring entirely due to the User’s breach of the v or due to the natural characteristics or inherent defects of the Goods; - The User fails to prove the sending, mishandling, or damage of the Goods; - Goods have been delivered and the recipient did not raise any objection upon receipt; - Goods are confiscated or destroyed in accordance with the law; - The User fails to comply with the complaint procedures as stipulated by Green SM/Partner and the law. 4.3.2.
