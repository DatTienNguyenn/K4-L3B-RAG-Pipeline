REGULATIONS ON THE USE OF GREEN SM DELIVERY

APPENDIX 10 – REGULATIONS ON THE USE OF GREEN SM DELIVERY (This Appendix is attached hereto and an integral part of the Terms of Use) 1. General Provisions The Application allows You to search for and connect with Partners for transporting goods by road motor vehicles (“Goods”). 2. Process for Partners Step 1: Switch to online mode and receive Orders. Step 2: Partner confirms the order acceptance from the Application. Step 3: Start delivery - Partner confirms the pickup on the Application. - Partner provides the Product to the User as requested on the Application. - Partner confirms successful or failed delivery on the Application. - Partner contacts the User to return goods for failed deliveries. Step 4: Payment - Partner receives payment from the User if the User chooses cash payment. Step 5: Complete the Order Complete the order, provide ratings, submit inquiries, complaints, or feedback on service quality (if any). Support Hotline: 1555 or 1900 2088 3. Regulations on Products 3.1. For Users/Customers 3.1.1. Must comply with and fully implement the Terms and Policies of Green SM when using the Service and Products; 3.1.2. The User must ensure: (a) Possess all legal documents related to the Goods and provide them to the Transport Partner and/or Green SM before transportation; (b) Properly package the Goods, fully and clearly mark symbols and codes, suitable for transportation in a closed vehicle compartment containing multiple packages without causing displacement, damage, or affecting other packages; (c) Deliver the Goods to the Transport Partner at the correct time and location. (d) Provide accurate information on the type of Goods, number of packages, dimensions, weight, and any specific requirements (if any). (e) Notify Green SM in advance if the Goods are bulky, heavy, or require loading/unloading assistance; any additional costs (if applicable) will be applied according to Green SM’s policies from time to time. (f) Provide complete proof of delivery, relevant documents, and strictly comply with packaging and information declaration regulations under Green SM’s Policy when filing a complaint. 3.1.3. You must not send any parcel containing any of the following items: (a) Narcotic drugs or psychotropic substances; (b) Precious metals (including gold, silver, etc. in the form of bars, ingots, coins, or similar forms); (c) Dangerous goods; (d) Firearms, weapons, ammunition, or military equipment; (e) Tobacco products; (f) Perishable goods (being items whose original condition or characteristics may deteriorate due to excessive changes in temperature, humidity, or time), except flowers and food products; (g) Obscene or reactionary publications, printed materials, or documents that are contrary to public security; (h) Flammable, explosive, unsanitary, or environmentally hazardous items or substances; (i) Items or goods prohibited from circulation or trading under applicable law; (j) Live animals; (k) Fur products; (l) Items, publications, or goods prohibited from being imported into Vietnam; (m) Vietnamese currency, foreign currency, and instruments having equivalent monetary value; (n) Fragile antiques; (o) Narcotic substances 3.1.4. Other goods as prescribed by applicable laws or as updated by Green SM from time to time. 3.1.5. Represent and warrant that You are the owner of the Goods or an authorized representative of the owner, and that You accept the Terms or are authorized to accept the Terms. 3.1.6. Be responsible for the accuracy and completeness of the Order and Goods information entered on the Application. Green SM and the Partner shall not be liable for delayed or failed deliveries caused by incorrect order information provided by You. 3.1.7. Represent and warrant that You have valid authorization from the recipient to provide their information to Green SM and the Partner via the Application or other methods. 3.1.8. Before starting delivery, You must inform the Partner of any specific precautions that need to be applied to handle the Goods according to their nature. 3.1.9. Represent and warrant that You have properly and fully packaged the Goods, arranged them neatly, and labeled and marked them. 3.1.10. Provide complete and feasible instructions to the Partner; otherwise, the Partner may refuse to deliver. 3.1.11. Be responsible for any delay in receiving goods or any loss, damage, contamination, pollution, or confiscation, or for any individual or organization directly or indirectly affected by You.. 3.1.12. An order is accepted only when all the following conditions are met: - Does not contain prohibited goods, dangerous goods, goods banned from transportation, or wildlife in Section 3.1.3 above as prescribed by law; - Includes complete sender and recipient information and other details required by the Application; - Has been received by the Partner on the Application; - Is properly packaged and wrapped as required (if any). 3.2. For Partners and Green SM 3.2.1. Partners and related parties must comply with and fully implement the Terms and Policies published by Green SM. 3.2.2. When fulfilling delivery requests, Partners must act responsibly, strictly observe traffic regulations, and ensure safety for themselves and others. 3.2.3. The Partner operating the vehicle is legally responsible for ensuring the safety of the vehicle. 3.2.4. Partners and Green SM reserve the right to refuse to provide the Product if the Goods are oversized and the Partner assesses that transporting them may violate traffic laws for vehicles on the road or compromise safety principles; or if the Goods are suspected to be prohibited or non-transportable under applicable laws. Accordingly, the Partner and/or Green SM have the right to inspect the Goods in such cases. 4. Compensation Liability 4.1. Value of Goods 4.1.1. The value of the Goods, as determined under this Section, serves as the basis for calculating the compensation liability of Green SM/Partner under Section 4.3 below. The value of the Goods is the amount stated/shown on the valid invoice issued by the seller to the User upon purchase of the Goods (“Invoice”), provided that the description of the Goods on the Invoice matches the description entered by the User on the Order and that the Invoice/document date precedes the time the User submits the order. 4.1.2. A valid invoice includes: - A Value-Added Tax (“VAT”) invoice meeting the validity conditions under applicable law, if the seller is an enterprise declaring VAT under the credit method; - A sales invoice, if the seller is an enterprise declaring VAT under the direct method; or a comparable reference value; - A customs declaration dossier, if the Goods are imported from abroad into Vietnam. 4.2. User’s Compensation Liability: Provided that Green SM is able to establish the User's fault and the resulting damage, the User must fully compensate Green SM, the Partner, and any related parties (if applicable) for all direct damages incurred in the following cases: - Damage occurs directly due to the User’s fault; - Goods fall under the category of prohibited or restricted goods for business/transportation under applicable laws; - Goods are improperly packaged, resulting in breakage and affecting other customers’ goods. 4.3. Compensation Liability of Green SM/Partner: 4.3.1. Principles of Compensation Green SM/Partner shall compensate based on the following principles: (i) Compensation is limited to direct damages and based on agreements related to the provision and use of the Product between Green SM/Partner and the User. Indirect damages, non-contractual damages, or lost profits due to non-compliant Product provision shall not be compensated. (ii) If the Goods are lost, damaged, or completely substituted, compensation will be made according to the regulations applicable to each type. (iii) The Customer/User must provide evidence demonstrating the fault and the damages, such as video recordings or images of the continuous packing process showing the type of Goods, quantity, weight, and other relevant details of the Goods that the Customer/User handed over to the Partner. (iv) No compensation shall be provided in the following cases: - No documentation confirming acceptance of the Goods; - No documentation proving the use of the Product; - Damage occurs entirely due to the User’s breach of the Contract or due to the natural characteristics or inherent defects of the Goods; - The User cannot prove the dispatch, deterioration, or damage of the Goods; - The Goods have been delivered and the recipient raised no objection upon receipt; - The Goods are confiscated or destroyed in accordance with the law; - The User fails to comply with the complaint regulations as prescribed by Green SM/Partner and applicable law. 4.3.2.
