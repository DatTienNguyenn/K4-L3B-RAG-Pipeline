DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION 1.1. “Fee Summary” means the summary of Service Fees and other costs payable by the Customer to GSM for each Reconciliation Period. 1.2. “Trip” means a transportation journey determined by the pickup point and one or more drop-off points, or the collection and delivery points of goods as established between the Driver and the Customer or User via the APP. 1.3. “Service” means transportation services connected via the APP, including but not limited to: (a) passenger transport by electric car, (b) passenger transport by electric motorcycle, (c) goods transport by electric motorcycle. GSM may adjust the Services and publish such adjustments on the APP and/or GSM Website. 1.4. “Limit” means the maximum outstanding debt GSM grants to the Customer under the Contract to use the Services before paying later. 1.5. “Reconciliation Period” means the period for aggregating and reconciling Service Fees, calculated from 00:00 hours on the 23rd day of the previous month to 22:59 hours on the 22nd day of the reconciliation month. 1.6. “Discount Rate” means the percentage (%) of commercial discount on Service Fees applied by GSM to the Customer for each type of Service as stipulated in the Contract. 1.7. ““User” means the person authorized by the Customer to directly use the Service under the Contract between GSM and the Customer. 1.8. “Vehicle” means electric cars with fewer than nine (9) seats and/or electric motorcycles owned and/or commercially exploited by GSM or GSM’s cooperating partners legally qualified for transportation business, which are used or approved by GSM to provide the Service. 1.9. “Management Account” means the Customer identification account provided by GSM for the Customer to manage and monitor User Accounts and the Green SM Card of the User. 1.10. “Application Account” means the identification account of the User, created and managed by the Management Account. For clarity, the User shall download and install the APP, register, and activate the account following the instructions on the APP. 1.11. “Driver” means the person operating the Vehicle to perform the Trip, having all legally required driving qualifications, registered and approved by GSM to participate in providing the Service. 1.12. “Green SM Card” means a physical card identifying the Customer/User established under the Contract. For clarity, the User shall use the Green SM Card to pay Service Fees upon completion of the Trip. 1.13. “PIN Code” means a six-digit code randomly generated by GSM’s system for a specific User linked to the Green SM Card, which the User provides to the Driver to verify and pay for the Trip using the Green SM Card. 2. SERVICE FEES, DISCOUNT RATE, DEPOSIT, RECONCILIATION AND PAYMENT 2.1. Deposit 2.1.1. To ensure compliance with and performance of the Contract, the Customer shall pay GSM a deposit as specified in Section 6, Clause 1 of the Contract (“Deposit”) within 10 Business Days from the Contract signing date and shall maintain the Deposit throughout the Term. 2.1.2. Within 30 days from the later of (i) the suspension of the Service, or (ii) the settlement of all outstanding debts, GSM shall return the Deposit to the Customer after deducting all amounts payable by the Customer to GSM (including but not limited to Service Fees, penalties, and other obligations under the Contract). The Deposit shall not bear interest during the term of the Contract and any dispute resolution period (if any). If the Deposit is less than the amount due, the Customer shall pay the outstanding balance upon GSM’s written request. 2.2. Service Fees 2.2.1. The Service Fee of each Trip shall be determined in accordance with GSM’s prevailing policy applicable to each type of Service and displayed on the APP. For clarity, when the User agrees to start a Trip, it is understood that the Customer/User accepts the Service Fee calculated by the APP, has read, fully understood, and agreed to the Terms of Use. 2.2.2. Service Fees: - Include VAT in accordance with applicable law and platform fees (if any). - Exclude tolls, ferry fees, and parking fees. Such costs are not included in the Service Fee for calculating the Discount Rate under Clause 2.3 below but will be reconciled together with the Service Fee. GSM or any entity authorized by GSM may pay the toll, ferry, or parking fees on behalf of the Customer for each Trip to the beneficiary, and GSM shall issue a VAT invoice for such fees, after which the Customer shall pay GSM together with the Service Fees pursuant to Clause 2.6 of these GT. 2.3. Discount Rate 2.3.1. The Discount Rate shall be applied to the Service Fee of each Trip as specified in the Contract. GSM may adjust the Discount Rate and notify the Customer in writing or via email at least 30 days prior to the effective date. 2.3.2. The discounted amount shall be calculated and recorded on the VAT invoice issued by GSM to the Customer. 2.4. Credit Policy 2.4.1. GSM agrees to apply a credit policy for the Customer with the Credit Limit specified in the Contract. This policy shall remain effective throughout the term of the Contract. 2.4.2. If the Service Fees and any additional charges (if any) incurred during a Reconciliation Period (before applying the Discount) exceed the Credit Limit, GSM has the right to temporarily suspend the Service. The Customer must either settle all outstanding debts with GSM or submit a request to increase the Credit Limit for that Reconciliation Period in writing, which must be approved by GSM to continue using the Service. For clarity, GSM has full discretion to accept or reject the Customer’s request. 2.5. Reconciliation 2.5.1. Within 02 Business Days (after the end of a Reconciliation Period or the completion of a Service Package), GSM shall prepare and send via email to the Customer’s contact person the Fee Summary (including any discounts under Clause 2.3 of this GT) for the previous Reconciliation Period or for the completed Service Package. The Fee Summary shall be determined based on the completed Trips, Service Fees, and other charges specified in Clause 2.2 of this TOU and recorded in GSM’s system. 2.5.2. The Customer shall have 3 days from the receipt of the Fee Summary to review and respond via email to GSM. The Customer may access the list of Trips and corresponding fees/costs through the Management Account. If GSM does not receive a response from the Customer within the above period, it shall be deemed that the Customer agrees with the Fee Summary, and GSM shall issue a VAT invoice to the Customer. 2.5.3. The Customer’s confirmation (or failure to respond within the time limit) of the Fee Summary as set out in Clause 2.5.2 shall be deemed as acceptance of the Service for the corresponding period by both Parties. 2.5.4. If the Parties cannot fully agree on the Fee Summary within the period specified in Clause 2.5.2, GSM shall issue the VAT invoice based on the Fee Summary sent by GSM. The Customer shall pay the full amount stated in the VAT invoice within the period set out in Clause 2.6.1 of this TOU. The Parties shall continue to discuss any unresolved amounts in the next Reconciliation Period, and any discrepancies (if any) shall be offset in the next Reconciliation Period and adjusted in the VAT invoice (if applicable). 2.6. Payment 2.6.1. The Customer shall pay GSM the Service Fees and other charges stated in the VAT invoice issued by GSM via bank transfer within 5 Business Days from the receipt of the invoice, but no later than the 15th day of each month. 2.6.2. GSM shall issue invoices to the Customer pursuant to Clause 2.5 of this TOU for all Service usage under the “Enterprise” profile on the APP, even if the User has already paid the Service Fees for a Trip at the end of the Trip using payment methods other than the Credit Limit. 2.6.3. If the Customer delays payment, the Customer shall pay interest on late payment calculated as follows: Late Payment Interest = Amount Due x 0.05% x Number of Days Late. Where: o Amount Due: the total Service Fees and any charges incurred under the invoice issued by GSM, minus the Deposit (if any); o Number of Days Late: the calendar days counted from the payment due date to the actual payment date to GSM. 3.
